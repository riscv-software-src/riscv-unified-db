<objective>
Close the complete Phase 1/2 executable evidence gap in one versioned successor
chain. Preserve accepted-v3 and all v3/v4/v5 history byte-for-byte. Freeze the
corrected semantic ontology and fixture gold, construct and independently accept
source-contract-v6, activate it through a validated v13 state chain, rerun the
adapter/formal/adversarial/H1 pipeline, and generate four fresh reports.

No task may infer human approval. Tasks 12, 14, and 17 stop for the exact human
decision they name. External publication is outside this plan.
</objective>

<execution_contract>
The executor runs tasks in numeric order. Every writer repeats its full read-only
preflight immediately before its first write. A writer may resume only when every
existing target is canonical and byte-exact; any divergent occupancy fails closed.
The current `phase2/source-authority.json` is mutable state: closure-v3 binds its
pre-state hash, while transition/readiness/revocation/integrity receipts bind its
pending and accepted hashes. Closure construction rejects ambient
`GIT_OPTIONAL_LOCKS`, `RUBYOPT`, or `RUBYLIB`, executes Git and Ruby with explicit
isolated environments, records exact argv, and records actual Git blob OIDs.
</execution_contract>
